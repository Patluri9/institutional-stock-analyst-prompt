# Quick Analysis Prompt (Lite Version)

> For fast analysis. Use the full prompt for deep research.

---

```
Act as an institutional equity analyst with web search. Analyze [TICKER]:

1. RETRIEVE LIVE DATA: current price, earnings beat/miss, guidance, analyst actions, material news (last 14 days), insider transactions, institutional ownership change.

2. THESIS: Business model, revenue drivers, AI/cloud exposure, TAM, valuation verdict — bull or bear in one sentence.

3. PEERS: Auto-identify top 3 competitors. Compare revenue growth, gross margin, FCF, forward P/E. Is [TICKER] the best play in the group?

4. FUNDAMENTALS: Earnings quality, SBC/dilution, balance sheet, customer concentration, red flags.

5. TECHNICALS: Price vs 21/50/200 EMAs. RSI, MACD, relative strength. Key support/resistance. Overextended or entry opportunity?

6. OPTIONS FLOW: Unusual activity, IV rank, put/call ratio, smart money signal.

7. TRADING PLAN:
   - Bias: Bullish / Neutral / Bearish
   - Entry: $X | Pullback zone: $X | Stop: $X | T1: $X | T2: $X | R/R: X:1

8. FINAL:
   - Bull case (2 bullets) | Bear case (2 bullets) | Hidden risk | Key catalyst
   - 3-month outlook | 1-year outlook | Conviction: X/10
   - Best strategy | Thesis invalidation

Concise bullets only. No disclaimers. No filler. Think hedge fund analyst.
```
